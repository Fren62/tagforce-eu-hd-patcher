# Texts drawn in the English textures: game original -> HD

Read **by eye** (2026-10-13): for every texture of `modules/en/ui_en`, the low-resolution European original of the ISO (enlarged) next to the HD PNG (sheets in `docs/changes_en/sheets/`, generated by `tools/analysis/texture_comparison_sheets.py`). There is no OCR on the PC: the table is transcribed by looking at the images. The HD rewrites the same words as the original; the differences are in **bold**.

Why so few textures compared with it/fr: the European original is already English, so there is nothing to translate. The English textures with the same hash as the US game (621) are already covered by MikeyMK's pack. Here there are only those that the US pack does **not** cover (different hash: European art or text) and that would otherwise stay enlarged with xBRZ.
